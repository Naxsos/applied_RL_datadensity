It keeps the same safety signal as the paper baseline, but learns the weight instead of freezing it. Across six seeds, that produced the strongest return / robustness balance.

What it shows
A better trade-off than baseline under the six-seed scoring analysis, with strong robustness to tuning.
What it does not prove
Whether lagr can match baseline's most aggressive safety setting (p=30) without more tuning runway.
Scope
These results aggregate six seeds per method/environment cell; keep the method choice separate from claims of generality.
