Goal02 / 06

What we wanted to verify

The LL environment is not just another task. It adds a risk region near the pad and a density signal that should identify unsafe near-ground states.

  • 01
    Low-density region is meaningful and matches risky states in the offline dataset.
  • 02
    Baseline and Lagrangian policies produce distinct behavior under the same safe RL pipeline.
  • 03
    Learned trajectories show whether the agent actually stabilizes and lands rather than just hovering.
Key idea: use the offline dataset to learn a density signal, then test whether the policy changes behavior in the intended near-ground risk region instead of only improving aggregate reward.
